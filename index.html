How it works

Three steps, and nothing moves until you approve it in your own wallet.

1

Type it

"Pay Tunde 20 USDC on Solana and Ada 15 USDC on Base." Use saved contact names or paste any address.

2

Check it

PayChat lists each payment with live prices and flags anything unclear in red, like an unknown name or an invalid address.

3

Approve once

One approval in your NEAR wallet sends every payment. Receipts update live until each one is delivered.

Pays out on the chains people actually use

You pay from NEAR with USDC or NEAR. Each recipient gets the token they asked for on their own network.

Built to stop mistakes before they cost you

Sending crypto to the wrong place usually can't be undone, so PayChat checks everything first.

  • Every address is checked against its network's format.
  • Names only resolve to contacts you saved yourself.
  • You see exactly what you'll send before you pay.
  • Recipients get the exact amount you typed.
  • If a payment fails, NEAR Intents refunds your NEAR account automatically.
  • Your keys never leave your wallet. PayChat has no server.
